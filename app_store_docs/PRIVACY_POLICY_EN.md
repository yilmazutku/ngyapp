# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** January 2026

The NGY Dietitian application ("App") is provided by Dietitian Nilay Göktepe Yılmaz ("we", "our", or "Service Provider"). This Privacy Policy explains how your personal data is collected, used, and protected when using the App.

## 1. Information We Collect

### 1.1 Information You Provide Directly

When using our App, we may collect the following information:

- **Account Information:** Name, surname, email address, phone number
- **Health Information:** Weight, height, body measurements, body fat percentage, and other nutrition-related data
- **Photos:** Meal photos and payment receipts (only when you share them)
- **Communication Content:** In-app messaging records
- **Appointment Information:** Appointment dates and notes
- **Payment Information:** Payment history and receipt images

### 1.2 Information Collected Automatically

- **Device Information:** Device type, operating system version
- **App Usage Data:** Login times, features used
- **Notification Tokens:** For delivering push notifications

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

- Providing dietitian services
- Preparing personalized diet plans
- Appointment management and reminders
- Communicating with you
- Tracking your nutrition progress
- Payment tracking
- Improving App performance
- Fulfilling legal obligations

## 3. Information Sharing

We do not share your personal information with third parties except in the following cases:

- **Legal Requirement:** When required by legal processes or government requests
- **Service Providers:** Limited sharing with technical infrastructure providers (Firebase/Google Cloud) necessary for the App's operation
- **With Your Consent:** When we have your explicit permission

### 3.1 Third-Party Services

Our App uses the following third-party services:

- **Firebase (Google):** For authentication, database, storage, and notifications
  - Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

## 4. Data Security

The security of your data is important to us. We implement the following security measures:

- Data is protected with SSL/TLS encryption during transmission
- Data is stored encrypted on secure servers
- Access is limited to authorized personnel only
- Regular security assessments are conducted

## 5. Data Retention

- Your personal data is retained for the duration of our service relationship
- If you delete your account, your data will be deleted within a reasonable period
- Some data may be retained longer due to legal requirements

## 6. Your Rights

You have the following rights regarding your personal data:

- **Right to Access:** Request information about what personal data we hold about you
- **Right to Rectification:** Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data
- **Right to Erasure:** Request deletion of your personal data
- **Right to Restriction:** Request restriction of processing of your data
- **Right to Data Portability:** Receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format
- **Right to Object:** Object to processing of your personal data
- **Right to Withdraw Consent:** Withdraw your consent at any time

To exercise these rights, please contact us using the contact information below.

## 7. Children's Privacy

Our App is not intended for individuals under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from individuals under 18. If you are a parent or guardian and become aware that your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us.

## 8. Device Permissions

Our App uses the following device permissions:

- **Camera:** To take meal photos
- **Photo Library:** To upload meal photos and receipts
- **Notifications:** For appointment reminders and message notifications

These permissions are only requested when you use the relevant features and can be declined.
